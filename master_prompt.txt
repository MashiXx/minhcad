============================================================

Use React Three Fiber / Three.js.

The model should have:

- realistic lighting
- subtle reflections
- controlled shadows
- dark environment
- technical blue grid

Camera should slowly move.

Example:

12. HERO MODEL INTERACTION
============================================================

============================================================
13. HERO SCROLL ANIMATION
============================================================

When user scrolls down:

The hero model should respond to scroll.

At scroll progress 0:

normal robot.

At 20%:

camera moves slightly closer.

At 40%:

robot rotates.

At 60%:

technical wireframe overlay begins appearing.

At 80%:

engineering dimensions / axis indicators appear.

At 100%:

transition toward the next section.

Use GSAP ScrollTrigger.

Animation must be tied to scroll progress.

Do NOT use a simple "fade in on scroll".
